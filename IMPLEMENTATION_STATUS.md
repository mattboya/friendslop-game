- Expand each wardrobe category from four to ten variants; visually inspect a broader sample of random combinations and add player-facing outfit selection.
- Human-play the full campsite → festival → extraction → reset loop, including seller discoverability, item selection, crowd trust balance and 8–12 minute pacing. See [campsite gameplay brief](docs/CAMPSITE_LOBBY_GAMEPLAY_BRIEF.md).

- Human assessment of role readability, hostility progression and goofy style at both crowd and first-person distances.
- Eight-player load, separate machines, network latency/loss/reconnect and frame-time profiling.
- Crowd performance remains open: the latest two-client macOS smoke measured 17.3 ms p95 during the rhythm challenge at 1280×720. Visibility culling, distance meshes and reduced distant animation are in place, but this measurement is above a 60 fps frame budget and needs target-hardware profiling.
- Human review of the HUD, menus and physical shop at multiple resolutions and input methods, plus performance profiling with bloom enabled on target hardware. Scripted mission captures use a 1280×720 macOS development player; the connection screen also has a 1440×900 layout capture.
- Bespoke surface textures, expressive dialogue faces, authored choreography, terrain variation, and tighter hand-object contact remain necessary to reach the supplied trailer's visual finish. The current native captures are an improved prototype, not a final art acceptance.
- Windows development/release builds and verification that release diagnostics are absent.
- Voice, Steam invitations and release integration; additional festival maps and progression content.
