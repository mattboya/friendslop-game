# After Hours — approved direction

Approved September 24, 2026. This supersedes conflicting pacing and progression details in the original prototype documents.

## Player promise

Two to eight friends enter a colorful festival, make questionable decisions, rescue each other, and catch the last shuttle. A run targets 8–12 minutes. Exploration toward an objective drives play; rhythm and rescue create interruptions and stories.

Some objectives require an affected player to interpret private clues while sober friends handle physical hazards. Roles are voluntary and can change between objectives. Failure escalates danger with time to intervene. Downed and detained players retain useful actions. Accidental chaos is welcome; repeated deliberate sabotage is limited.

Complete the objective and extract at least one survivor to advance. Extra survivors earn a shared bonus without removing earned objective rewards. Long-term rewards are festival unlocks, cosmetics, and lateral options, never permanent power. Later festivals moderately increase rhythm difficulty and increase teamwork/danger; optional performances remain harder. Replays preserve recognizable maps and vary circumstances.

## Immediate scope

One festival demonstrating the complete clue → performance → friend rescue → shuttle loop. Additional festival maps are future content. Deliver original character source assets and a native testable build. Distinguish automated checks, runtime evidence, and human fun/quality acceptance.

## Visual references

- [RV There Yet?](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3949040/RV_There_Yet/): stylized outdoor shapes and warm lighting in the observed store footage.
- [Gamble With Your Friends](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AEur1h1kkK0): user-supplied gameplay footage sampled September 25. Its simple character forms feel finished through saturated color, practical light sources, physical controls, clear interaction feedback and a compact HUD. The [store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3892270/Gamble_With_Your_Friends/) remains a secondary reference.
- [How to Fish](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4001890/How_to_Fish/): visibly faceted characters, exaggerated faces, first-person physical interactions in the observed store footage.

These are inspiration, not asset sources. Store pages and sampled gameplay were inspected; no claim of playing these games. User explicitly wants intentionally goofy animation.

Our interpretation: lanky festival attendees, uneven shoulders, oversized hands/shoes, readable eyes and noses, bucket hats, saturated clothing, warm lanterns against cool dusk. Use authored/procedural body poses for offbeat dancing, shuffling, panic, reaching, and crawling. Keep camera and authoritative movement steady. Assess quality by expressive silhouettes, grounded feet, readable reactions, and consistent lighting, not polygon count.

The September 25 polish pass translates that reference into a dusk sky, warm camp path lighting, restrained emissive stage edges, a small bloom halo, a compact in-play HUD, and larger shop cards with clear prices and page controls. Keep the original goofy festival identity. Character expressions, physical item handling, hand contact and a measured performance pass still need further work.
