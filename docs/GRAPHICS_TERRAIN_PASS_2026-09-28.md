# Stage-route terrain checkpoint — September 28, 2026

This is progress within the [graphics upgrade plan](GRAPHICS_UPGRADE_PLAN_2026-09-27.md), not acceptance of its finished playable sample or reference-quality target.

## Changes

- The 58 m main stage approach now uses a shallow, irregular visual mesh and matching worn shoulders. Its flat gameplay collider and generated NavMesh remain independent of the decoration.
- The original 1024² dirt map has broad pressed-earth patches, scattered boot marks, small gravel and straw. The route has fifteen repeats along its length, with existing mipmaps and 4× anisotropic filtering retained. Crosspaths and the camp share the new map at their own scale.
- The festival grass is one combined visual mesh in place of individual cube renderers. Explicit upward normals keep its double-sided blades from going black under dusk light. The grass has no collision and stays out of the main route and attraction clearances.
- Eight existing perimeter trees now frame the stage and grove approaches at varied positions and sizes. Total tree count is unchanged. A development-build-only diagnostic records the generated grass blade count.

The dirt is generated by `scripts/generate_festival_surfaces.py` and published through the staged art pipeline. `FestivalDirt.png` keeps its Unity GUID. The trees reuse the existing original Blender exports.

## Verification and limits

`node scripts/verify-project.mjs`, `node scripts/unity.mjs test-edit`, `test-play`, `build-mac-development`, and `build-mac-release` passed. The final development build passed `FESTIVAL_GRAPHICS_PROFILE=1 node scripts/native-smoke.mjs`, including the two-client mission and connection UI capture. The final client reported 17.4 and 17.1 ms rolling frame p95 at two populated festival sample points, with 12.7 and 12.1 ms GPU frames and 1,004 and 1,164 visible renderers. This is a short scripted run at 1280×720 Ultra on one Mac with both clients; seeds and workload vary. It does not establish a performance improvement, sustained 60 fps, Windows minimum hardware, or normal human traversal. The scripted smoke teleports between major locations.

Next review should inspect the complete route during a human-paced session, then continue object and material finishing around the clinic, security and shuttle and assess sustained cost before adding dense scenery.
