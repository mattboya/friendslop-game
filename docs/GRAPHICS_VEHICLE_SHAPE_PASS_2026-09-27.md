# Campsite vehicle shape checkpoint — September 27, 2026

This is a checkpoint within the [graphics upgrade plan](GRAPHICS_UPGRADE_PLAN_2026-09-27.md). It does not complete the M2 sample or meet the supplied trailer's visual quality target.

## Design and source

The previous [van view](graphics-grove-2026-09-27/van-native.png) showed a rear window detached from the cab shell. The earlier [car view](graphics-camp-2026-09-27/car.png) showed a short cabin roof and steep rear slope that read as a toy hatchback. The vehicle recipe now uses two distinct, original silhouettes: a compact wagon with a long roof and upright hatch, and a two-tone camper with a continuous upper cab and sloped front windscreen. The existing campsite placement, door anchors, collision proxies and gameplay interiors remain in place.

[A period camper photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Volkswagen_Type_2_camper.jpg) and [a station wagon photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1988-1991_Volvo_240_GL_station_wagon_(2008-12-16).jpg) were inspected for broad construction and proportions. The project does not include those photographs, vehicle logos, or copied geometry. All exported meshes are generated by the original Blender script.

The van cab is a single YZ-profile extrusion that overlaps the lower body; the roof skin touches that shell. The recipe checks the windscreen's lower and upper offsets against the sloped cab plane and the rear pane's offset against the back face. The wagon's side glazing, roof rail and upright rear hatch follow its extended roof profile; its recipe checks the rear pane against the hatch slope. A six-direction neutral Workbench turnaround is reproducible with `scripts/render_world_asset_turnaround.py`.

The final published source, manifest, and both FBXs passed project static checks, Unity validation, and a macOS development build. The two-client native mission and connection-menu capture passed. A macOS release build and release diagnostic exclusion check also passed. The saved gameplay captures establish that the new geometry and material render in the native player, not just Blender.

The 1280×720 Ultra two-process run reported rolling p95 frame intervals of 8.9 ms in camp and 15.7–16.7 ms at populated festival points on this Mac. These are point samples on a new seed, not a controlled before/after benchmark or a minimum Windows PC result. The festival still has no established 60 fps margin.

## Still open

The vehicles now have more coherent contact and recognizable wagon/camper shapes, but their paint, glass, tires, interior spaces and close silhouettes still read as stylized prototype art. The surrounding ground, trees, architecture, crowd motion and large HUD cards also fall short of the reference's finish. Inspect normal walking views and vehicle entry at a human pace, then continue the wider scene, material and lighting work before visual acceptance.
