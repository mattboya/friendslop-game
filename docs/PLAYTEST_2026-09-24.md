# One-festival playtest

## Run

Open `Builds/macOS/Development/FestivalCoop.app` and choose CREATE GAME. You enter the campsite immediately and may play alone or have a friend JOIN GAME using your LAN address. For two local windows:

```sh
node scripts/launch-local-clients.mjs --players 2
```

Mouse looks, WASD moves, Shift runs, E performs the focused action, Escape opens or closes the compact pass, Tab shows landmarks, arrows perform rhythm, and 1/2/3 select inventory. Click the intended window before controlling it.

The camp has cars, tents, two central shade shelters and a porta potty. Look at a physical prop on one of the shelves beside the seller and press E to carry one copy. You can keep moving while it is unpaid; press G to return it. Bring it to the seller near (0, 7), then press E twice to present it and confirm the price. Purchases draw from shared stock and appear in your pockets. Little Spoon is the cheapest item and is worn as a necklace with no active button action. When equipped, walk to the lit trailhead at (0, 19) and press E to ready; your character dances in place. E unreadies you. Once all connected players are ready, a five-second countdown starts the festival automatically. Unpaid props return to the shelf at launch. After results, the host chooses NEXT CAMP on the pass to reset.

At the festival Night Market, look at a shelf prop and press E twice to view and confirm its price. The item goes straight into your pockets without an unpaid carry step. Escape opens the festival pass for Resume, Crew + Nearby, Settings, and Leave Session.

## Play the new objective

1. Visit NIGHT MARKET at (-18, -22). One player selects the free clue tasting. This voluntary effect lasts 90 seconds and creates no resellable item. With a crew, one teammate stays sober.
2. The affected player sees a private hint and floating ring above the correct Sun or Moon totem. With a crew, read the hint aloud and keep a sober partner within four metres for the three-second interpretation. Alone, hold at the correct totem for six seconds.
3. Interpret the second totem together. If an effect expires, return to the market or use an owned consumable. Clue order varies with the round seed.
4. Dance with a festivalgoer and score at least 60%. The beat and note lane provide timing. A failed performance raises local suspicion and can be retried. Optional poi and DJ performances have twelve notes at a faster beat spacing.
5. Search the northern/side routes for the missing friend. Recruit them with E. This commits $20 to the current round's shared stash.
6. Escort the friend to LAST SHUTTLE at (0, -32), regroup, and extract. Each additional living, connected player within five metres earns the team $10. The first rescue cosmetic persists. The ten-minute clock ends the round if you run out of time.

If downed, crawl toward help and use the distraction action every four seconds to open a short rescue opportunity. Friends can drag you away and revive you. If you die while playing alone, travel as a spirit to the medical tent at (24, -20) and complete a 15-second self-revival task; this is available twice per round. If detained, use four spaced distraction actions to escape or have friends complete the release task. Prism tabs and Moon caps add a bounded movement slowdown and wandering step while active; the affected player sees first-person distortion and teammates see changed eyes and posture. Q uses the selected item, and G drops it.

## Reproduce verification

```sh
node scripts/test-domain.mjs all
node scripts/verify-project.mjs
node scripts/unity.mjs test-edit
node scripts/unity.mjs test-play
node scripts/unity.mjs build-mac-development
node scripts/native-solo-smoke.mjs
node scripts/native-smoke.mjs
```

The domain runner can use Unity's bundled Roslyn compiler and runtime when a standalone .NET SDK is absent. The solo native runner checks camp purchase, intoxication cues, clues, conversation, distraction, dance, death, revival and success. The two-client runner starts and stops its own development players on port 17779, reserves separate Little Spoon copies and pays at the camp counter on both peers, verifies the ready countdown and direct market purchase, checks that only the affected client sees the clue ring while the helper sees their changed face, completes the mission through the client command channel, and renders camp, market, pass, and character views. It also clicks CREATE GAME from the main menu and confirms the campsite appears. Actor positions are scripted between stages; human traversal, camera aiming at props, and timing fairness under network latency still need testing.

## Decisions and limitations

- Original Blender sources: `ArtSource/FestivalCharacter.blend`, `FestivalHands.blend` and `FestivalWorld.blend`; regenerate through `scripts/generate_modular_character.py`, `generate_festival_hands.py` and `generate_festival_world_assets.py`.
- The shared 13-bone rig has three body shapes and four variants in each of eight wardrobe slots. Attendee combinations are deterministic per actor; first-person hands match body, skin and sleeves. Three dance styles, smoothed pose changes and task reactions keep body comedy separate from movement authority. Facial expression and hand-contact animation still need work.
- The map now uses original Blender stage, vendor, clinic, security, shuttle, tree, tent and totem meshes. The separate camp has original Blender car, shade and porta potty meshes plus a visible seller; the Little Spoon has a wearable mesh and pickup model. Primitive geometry still supplies paths, proxy collision, grass and some signs. The shuttle shell has a visible interior, while the extraction action currently completes at the entrance.
- The Sun and Moon totems use smaller, distinct sculptural forms. The clue ring is local presentation driven by the viewer-filtered hint; it is never sent as shared world state. The HUD uses separate objective, player and crew cards plus a compact action prompt. Notice cards disappear after five seconds.
- One festival is implemented. Additional festival maps, persistent festival unlocks and lateral equipment unlocks remain future work. Current money is round-local; only the existing rescue cosmetic persists.
- The audible pulse is a timing aid, not a finished soundtrack. Proximity voice and Steam invitations are not implemented.
- Two native processes completed the scripted cooperative mission on this Mac. Eight-player load, other machines, network impairment, Windows, Steam packaging and full human playtesting remain unverified.
- Windows Build Support is not installed. No new external tools, plugins or skills were installed.
