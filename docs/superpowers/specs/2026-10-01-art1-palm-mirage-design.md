## Goal

Make festival 1, Palm Mirage, read as a goofy parody of a desert polo-field festival: palm rows, a bright lawn, desert mountains, a real Ferris wheel, a dressed-up main stage and three signature landmarks. Also replace the primitive placeholders the waves left behind, and fix the stale PRISM labels. When the work is done, Matt gets a short list of anything he must do himself for the repo to use the new art.

Success is a native build where Palm Mirage is recognisable at a glance, by day and by night, and nothing about play has changed: same layout, same colliders, same sight lines, same tests passing. Ember Playa looks exactly as it does today.

## Constraints

1. **No gameplay change.** Core is not touched, `FestivalSession.ProtocolVersion` stays where it is, and no collider is added or moved. Line of sight is a host raycast against colliders (`HasLineOfSight`, `FestivalTwists.cs:49`), and the crowd sim knows nothing of new obstacles. So every new mesh is visual only (`FestivalArtView.Create` already strips colliders), and anything big sits outside the play area or above head height.
2. **Ember Playa is unchanged.** All Palm Mirage dressing is shown only when `FestivalIndex == Festivals.PoloFestival`, the same switch that `FestivalTwistVisuals.cs:70` uses today. The pre-level camp is shared by both festivals and is out of scope.
3. **Style.** Follow `docs/APPROVED_DIRECTION.md` and `docs/BLENDER_ASSET_BRIEF.md`: goofy, faceted and readable, not premium or realistic. Every design is original. The astronaut is our own chunky character, not a copy of any real sculpture.
4. **Wave isolation.** Work happens only in `/Users/mboyajian/Code/friendslop-game`, never in `.worktrees/`. Modeling can be committed now. The C# wiring waits until wave 2026-10-01 has landed (see Merge path).
5. **Budgets** (triangles, after the kit's merge). Palm ≤ 2,000 each (current trees are 1,490–3,132). Wheel: base + rotor + 8 gondolas ≤ 6,000. Stage dressing ≤ 6,000. Astronaut ≤ 8,000. Tower ≤ 6,000. Canopy ≤ 4,000. Mountains ≤ 6,000 in total. Each creature ≤ 1,200. Giggle Tank ≤ 1,000. Balloon ≤ 300. VIP board ≤ 500. Frame time in the native smoke must stay within 5% of the current build's.

## Assets

All are generated by `scripts/generate_polo_dressing.py` (`CONFIG` on its first lines, `festival_kit` helpers). The outputs are `ArtSource/PoloDressing.blend`, `ArtSource/polo-dressing-manifest.json`, FBX files in `Assets/Festival/Art/Resources/` and review galleries in `artifacts/polo-dressing/`. Each model's pivot is its base centre at y = 0 unless noted.

| Resource | What it is | Size and fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `FestivalPalmTall` | Straight palm for the formal rows: ringed trunk, frond crown, a few hanging dates | 9–11 m, scaled per spot in code |
| `FestivalPalmLean` | Curved, leaning palm for the outer grove and approach spots | 8–10 m |
| `FestivalWheelBase` | A-frame legs and a boarding platform | Legs at x ±1.1, z ±3.2, meeting at the axle at 7.5 m, matching `FestivalTwistVisuals.Wheel()` |
| `FestivalWheelRotor` | Rim, 8 spokes, hub; pivot at the axle; rim in the local y–z plane | Radius 6. Rim segments alternate StageGlowMint and StageGlowRose, as now |
| `FestivalWheelGondola` | One open gondola with a canopy; pivot at its hanging point | Fits the current 1.2 × 0.9 × 1.0 m block and hangs 0.75 m below the rim |
| `FestivalStageMirage` | Two side video screens, an arched crown and a `PALM MIRAGE` sign | Stays inside x ±11, z 27.5–36.5. It sits above the existing stage colliders or inside them, and leaves the takeover deck (0, 26) and the DJ positions clear. The `AFTER HOURS` sign stays |
| `FestivalPetalCanopy` | Two masts carrying 5–7 big fabric petals that reach forward over the dance area | Masts at (±10, 31), radius ≤ 0.3, so below 4 m they sit inside the speaker proxies (2 × 4 × 2). Every petal is ≥ 6 m above the ground, and the canopy reaches no further south than z ≈ 20 |
| `FestivalRainbowTower` | A spiral of coloured panels on a scaffold, wider at the top | About 20 m tall, footprint ≤ 6 × 6 m |

| `FestivalDesertRidge1..3` | Low-poly ridge segments that ring the far horizon | 25–60 m tall on a ring of about 180–260 m. They must stay visible through Palm Mirage's daytime haze: the plan checks the fog end distance and the camera's far clip, and chooses the ring radius from them |
| `FestivalGiggleTank` | Tank body, shoulder, band, valve and hand wheel | Same sizes and heights as the primitives in `FestivalGiggleTank.cs:22-27`; same colours |
| `FestivalGiggleBalloon` | One balloon with a tied knot | Tinted per `BalloonLook` in code |
| `FestivalVipBoard` | Board and gold trim | As wide as the stall poles (`FestivalTwistVisuals.cs:36-37`); the lettering stays a TextMesh |
| `FestivalCreatureGnome`, `…Pixie`, `…Dragon`, `…MushroomSprite`, `…Jackalope` | VISION-2's creatures as real low-poly models | Each matches the bounds of its current primitive parts (about 0.4 m), feet at the origin, facing +z. Parts are named by paint slot (`__P0`…`__P10`) so `FestivalCreatures` keeps assigning its own `Paints` |

**PRISM labels.** These are fixed at their source, not in the new generator:
- `generate_festival_world_assets.py:1317`: the tin label `PRISM` becomes `STAMPS`.
- `finish_festival_world_package.py:69`: the banner `PRISM SUPPLY` becomes `GOOD TIMES SUPPLY`.

Only the affected FBX are replaced: the stock tin, `FestivalStockPrism` (built from the tin by `generate_festival_dressing.py`), and the stock stall. Any other regenerated output is thrown away, and the diff must show only those files. Resource names, including `FestivalStockPrism`, stay as they are, so `FestivalSession.cs` is untouched.

**Palette.** New colour keys `Frond`, `PalmBark` and `Sand` (the astronaut's visor uses the existing `Glass`). Each one is added with identical values to `KIT_CONFIG.pal` (`festival_kit.py`) and to the colour table in `FestivalArtView.cs`. `PalmBark` uses the `FestivalBark` texture and `Frond` uses `FestivalLeaf`. The creature paint slots stay in `FestivalCreatures.Paints`.
